# pdf actions

The `pdf` MCP tool, action by action, as built in formpdf 0.13.0. The action names formsld's `pdf` tool used (info, text, images, tables, merge, split, header_footer, crop, find, redact, to_docx) are unchanged; `to_deck` is replaced by `to_pptx`, and the workbench's own operations are added. All paths are absolute. A failure answers `pdf [action] failed: [reason]` with isError.

## Where an action works

| Arguments | Source | Changing actions write |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `path` | that file | `[stem]-[suffix].pdf` beside it, or `out` (file or existing folder) |
| `doc` (id or key) | that open PDF | a new version, shown live in the window as your turn |
| neither | the window's open PDF, else the last PDF you opened | as with `doc` |

Suffixes: header-footer, numbered, watermarked, cropped, redacted, organized, protected, unlocked, metadata, compressed, clean, filled, commented, form, merged, linked, attached, measured, spelling, edited.

## Page syntax

`pages`: `"3"`, `"1-4"`, `"2,5"`, `"1-3,7"`, `"4-"`; omit for every page. `ranges` (split): one string per file, open ends allowed (`"4-"`, `"-3"`, `"all"`). Coordinates are points from the top left of the page as shown.

## Read actions

- **info**: JSON with bytes, encrypted, locked, title, author, subject, keywords, creator, producer, created, modified, pages, sizes `[{w, h, rotate}]`, outline, form field count.
- **text** `{pages}`: `## Page N` sections, cut at 16,000 characters. Scanned pages have no text.
- **images** `{pages}`: one line per picture `p1 · img_... · 1920x1080 · image/jpeg`; files in `~/Formpdf/Media`. Pictures under 24 px are skipped.
- **tables** `{pages}`: each ruled or clearly aligned table as `pN:` and a markdown table (header and 30 rows).
- **find** `{query | preset, regex, caseSensitive, wholeWord, pages}`: presets `email`, `phone`, `card` (Luhn checked), `date`. Answers `N matches on M pages` and up to 200 `pN · match · context` lines; structured `matches` with `rects` (first 500).
- **comments**: existing comments with page, kind, author, status and replies.
- **fields**: form fields with name, type, page, value and options.
- **hidden**: counts of metadata, javascript, attachments, comments, fields, layers, links, bookmarks, thumbnails, private, hidden_text.
- **compare** `{other, otherIsNewer, pictures}`: pages changed, added, removed and the same, then up to 200 differences with before and after text. Nothing changes.
- **links** `{pages}`: every link with page, index, box and target (web address, email or page). Use page and index to remove one.
- **attachments** `{save: name, out}`: files attached to the document and files pinned to pages (page, index, name, size, description). With `save`, writes that file to `out` or `~/Formpdf/Exports`.
- **measurements**: measurement comments (distance, perimeter, area) with page, index, value and unit, read from their stored scale.
- **snap_points** `{pages}`: path ends, segment midpoints and crossings on the page, in points from the top left, for exact measuring.
- **spell** `{scope: "comments,fields,pages", texts: [{id, text}], ignore: [words], lang, skip_caps, suggest}`: possible misspellings in the PDF's comments, form text fields, page text (scope) or texts you pass, each with where it is (comment page and index, field name, page, or your id), offset, context and up to six suggestions. Default scope is comments and fields. Words in all capitals, web and email addresses and the person's own dictionary are skipped.
- **items** `{pages}`: text and pictures added by formpdf (id, kind, page, x, y, w, h, rot, flips, z order; for text the font and paragraphs; for pictures the crop) and pictures already on the page (page, op, box, pixel size), and `paragraphs`: text already on the page grouped into paragraphs (page, key, box, rot, font with the document's font name, paragraphs, text, editable). Use the id, page and op, or page and paragraph key with edit_items; `text: false` leaves paragraphs out.
- **blocks** `{}`: the document in reading order as `b1 p3 list: text` lines (kinds h1 to h3, bold, para, list; wrapped lines joined, page numbers dropped) and pictures as `p1 p1 picture 750x500 px`. The ids feed compose. On a PDF made by compose it lists the blocks of the document it was made from.
- **compose** `{max_pages, blocks: [{kind, ref | refs | text | items, label, level, width}], size, min_size, margin, color}`: rebuilds the open PDF from chosen blocks, in order, as editable text and pictures: title, heading, subheading, text (ref, refs or text), bullets and steps (items: `"b4-b9"` or `{ref | text, label, level}`; steps are numbered and keep counting across pages; a label is a bold lead-in), picture (ref p1, width as a share of the text width or in points). Headings stay with what follows; the largest text size from `size` (11.5) down to `min_size` (7) that fits `max_pages` is used, then the picture shrinks. Use it to keep only some parts, relabel, reorder, shrink a picture or fit N pages in one call, instead of deleting content piece by piece and retyping it.
- **signatures**: every digital signature with its verdict (`valid`, `unknown` when the certificate is not trusted on this computer, `invalid`), signer, signing time, timestamp, whether it certifies, and plain sentences saying what changed after it (comments, form filling, signing, other changes) and whether that was allowed; then empty signature fields and, for a prepared request, each signer as signed, next or waiting. Validation never uses the network.
- **accessibility**: 32 PDF/UA and WCAG accessibility checks in seven groups (Document, Page content, Forms, Alternate text, Tables, Lists, Headings) plus a Tag correctness group (heading levels against their size, headings tagged as paragraphs, reading order against the page, placeholder alternate text, language of each part, right-to-left text drawn in visual order without ActualText), each `passed`, `failed`, `manual` or `skipped` (structure checks need tags), with a sentence, `findings` (page, text, element `id`) and the `fix` that repairs it, if any. `fixes` lists the fixes fix_accessibility can apply.
- **verapdf** `{flavour: 1a | 1b | 2a | 2b | 2u | 3a | 3b | 3u | ua1 | ua2}`: runs veraPDF, the reference validator for PDF/A and PDF/UA, when it is installed (read verapdf_status first; formpdf does not ship it). Without `flavour` it checks the standard the file claims, PDF/UA when it claims both. Result: `compliant`, `profile`, `version` and `rules`, each failed rule with `clause`, `test`, `failed` (checks) and `pages`. Use it after pdfa or claim_ua to confirm the result with an outside tool; it does not cover PDF/X or PDF/VT.
- **structure** `{pages, only: "figures"}`: tagged regions in reading order: `id` (use with edit_structure), page, order on the page, type (P, H1, L, Table, Figure, Link, Form and the rest), the tag as written, text, alternate text and box; `figures` lists every figure with its alternate text. Untagged PDFs answer `tagged: false`.
- **print_layout** (same arguments as print_pdf): the sheets the print layout makes, each with its size and cells (page, placed box, clip, quarter turn, scale) in points from the top left, so you can say how many sheets and how they look before printing.
- **pdfa_check** `{level: 2b | 2u | 3b}`: problems against PDF/A with the clause of ISO 19005, the pages, and whether converting fixes each (`fixable`); `conforms` and what the file claims to be (`claims`).
- **preflight** `{profile: print | office | screen}`: checks fonts, picture resolution at the printed size, colour spaces, spot colours, transparency, hairlines, trim boxes and scripts; each `pass`, `fail`, `warn` or `info` with findings and the `fix` for preflight_fix.
- **ai_ask** `{question, pages}`, **ai_summary** `{length: short | medium | long, pages}`: the answer from the AI provider the person chose in Preferences > AI, citing `[p. N]` with quotations checked against the pages; result `answer` and `citations` (`page`, `quote`, `verified`). Off until a provider is chosen; with "your agent" as provider, answer the person's questions with answer_question instead, and for your own questions read the text yourself.
- **ai_outline** `{slides (3..30, default 8), brief, paths}`: a presentation outline drafted from this PDF and up to ten more files (PDFs, Office files through LibreOffice, .txt or .md); result `outline` `{title, subtitle, slides: [{title, bullets, notes, sources: [{doc, page}]}]}`.

## Change actions

- **header_footer** `{header: {left, center, right}, footer, size, color, font: sans | serif | mono, margins: {top, bottom, left, right}, pages, start, apply: all | odd | even, bates: {prefix, start, digits, suffix}, date}`. Tokens `<<page>>`, `<<pages>>`, `<<date>>`, `<<file>>`, `<<bates>>`; Bates numbers go at `<<bates>>`, else the footer's right.
- **page_numbers** `{position: bottom-center | bottom-right | bottom-left | top-center | top-right | top-left, format: "{n}" | "Page {n}" | "Page {n} of {total}" | "{n} / {total}", start, size, color, pages}`.
- **watermark** `{text, opacity (0..1, default 0.15), angle (45), size (64), color, pages}`.
- **crop** `{margins: {l, t, r, b} | box: {x, y, w, h} | auto: true, pages}`: sets the CropBox; the hidden area stays in the file.
- **redact** `{query | preset (+ find options) | areas: [{page, x, y, w, h}] | marked: true, color, overlay, mode: content | raster, pages}`: true redaction. The text, pictures (only the covered pixels), shapes, comments and form fields under each box are removed from the file and a box in `color` (default black) with optional `overlay` text is drawn; the rest of the page stays text. Redaction marks saved in the file are applied too; `marked: true` applies only those. The call stops and writes nothing if a check finds anything left under a box. `mode: raster` turns the marked pages into 200 dpi pictures instead, for scans. No match writes nothing.
- **mark_redactions** `{areas: [{page, x, y, w, h}], color, overlay}`: writes redaction marks (Redact annotations) without removing anything, so the person can review them in the window; `redact {marked: true}` applies them.
- **organize** `{order: [pages in the new order; pages left out are deleted], delete: [pages], rotate: 90 | 180 | 270 (with pages, default all), blank: [positions after which a blank page goes; 0 for the front]}`.
- **protect** `{password, owner, permissions: {print, copy, modify, annotate, fill, assemble}}`: AES-256.
- **unlock** `{password}`.
- **metadata** `{title, author, subject, keywords, strip}`.
- **compress** `{quality: 10..95, default 70}`: recompresses pictures, compresses streams, merges identical objects.
- **sanitize** `{remove: [...]}`: removes the named kinds of hidden information (see hidden); fields are flattened so values stay. Default: metadata, javascript, attachments, private, thumbnails.
- **fill** `{values: {field name: value}, flatten}`: use fields first for the names.
- **annotate** `{annotations: [...], highlight, color, flatten}`: annotations are `{page, kind, color, text, x, y, w, h}` for note, freetext, rect, ellipse; `{page, kind: "callout", text, x, y, w, h, anchor: [x, y]}` for a text box whose arrow points at anchor; `{page, kind, rects: [[x, y, w, h]]}` for highlight, underline, strikeout, squiggly; `{page, kind, points: [[x, y], ...], fill}` for polygon and cloud (3 or more points, closed) and polyline (2 or more, open); `{page, kind, points: [[x, y], [x, y]]}` for line and arrow. `highlight: "phrase"` highlights every match.
- **form** `{create: [{type: text | multiline | checkbox | radio | dropdown | listbox | date | signature, page, x, y, w, h, name, options}], rename: {old: new}, delete: [names]}`.
- **merge**: with `paths` and `out` (and no path or doc) merges those files into `out`; the paths may also be pictures and Office files (Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OpenDocument, RTF, text, CSV), converted in order, and then one file is enough; with `path` and `paths` writes `[stem]-merged.pdf`; on the open PDF appends `paths` to it live.
- **edit_links** `{add: [{page, x, y, w, h, to}], remove: [{page, index}], auto, border}`: `to` is a web address, `mailto:` address or page number. `auto: true` links every web and email address found in the text. Run links first for indexes.
- **edit_attachments** `{add: [{path, name, description, page, x, y}], remove: [names], unpin: [{page, index}]}`: without `page` the file is attached to the document; with `page` it is pinned there as a paperclip comment. A name already taken becomes `name (2).ext`.
- **edit_outline** `{ops: [{op, index, title, page}]}`: bookmarks. `op` is add, rename, delete, dest, up, down, indent or outdent; `index` is the bookmark's position in info's `outline` list (pre-order, 0 first). add without index appends at the top level, with index inserts after that bookmark; rename takes `title`; dest and add take `page`. Ops run in order, each against the outline as the previous one left it. Other bookmarks keep their destinations and styles.
- **measure** `{add: [{page, kind: distance | perimeter | area, points: [[x, y], ...], unit, factor, label, color}], remove: [{page, index}]}`: measurement comments other readers show. `factor` is units per point (true size for the unit when omitted); distance takes two points, area at least three.
- **spell_fix** `{fixes: [{where: comment, page, index, nm, offset, word, to} | {where: field, name, offset, word, to}]}`: replaces each word in comment text or a field value, using spell's output. Text boxes are redrawn with the corrected text.
- **ocr** `{dpi: 300, skip_text: true, deskew: true, redo: false, pages}`: recognises scanned pages on this computer and adds invisible, searchable text (render mode 3, one text matrix per word, an embedded Unicode font) that find, redact, text, to_docx and to_pptx then use. Pages with real text, or recognised before, are skipped unless `skip_text: false` or `redo: true`; tilted full-page scans are straightened (the scan itself is replaced by the straightened picture). About 10 seconds a page. `get_context` says `OCR: not installed` with the reason when it cannot run. Result: `recognised`, `words`, `straightened`, `skipped`.
- **sign** `{identity, password, field | box: {page, x, y, w, h}, reason, location, contact, picture, certify: "forms" | "comments" | "none", lock, timestamp}`: signs with a digital ID the person made or imported in the window (Fill Forms and Sign > Sign with a digital ID). Only sign when the person asked and gave you the ID password in this conversation; never guess one. `field` names an empty signature field (see signatures); otherwise `box` places a new one. `picture` is a data URL shown left of the text. `timestamp: true` uses the server set in Preferences. Saved as an incremental update, so earlier signatures stay valid. Result: `field`, `timestamped`.
- **request_signatures** `{signers: [{name, email}], message}`: assigns the PDF's empty signature fields to the signers in document order (each field's tooltip names its signer) and records the request; signatures then reports who is next. The PDF needs one empty signature field per signer first (form action, type signature). The person sends the PDF themselves.
- **autotag** `{lang, title}`: tags the PDF for screen readers from its layout: headings (renumbered from H1 without gaps), paragraphs, lists, tables with header cells, figures, links, form fields and comments in reading order, with running headers, footers and page numbers marked as background. Replaces existing tags (and the alternate text in them); refused on a signed PDF. Then run accessibility, add alternate text with edit_structure, and check the order. Later edits to page content are not tagged until autotag runs again. Result: `elements`, `headings`, `lists`, `tables`, `figures`, `annotations`.
- **fix_accessibility** `{fixes: [tag, title, lang, tabs, annotations, descriptions, summaries, bookmarks, headings, spans, rtl], lang, title}`: applies the named fixes (all but headings when omitted): tags when there are none, the title shown in the window title, the language, tab order, comments and fields missing from the tags (and link descriptions), field descriptions from their labels, table summaries, bookmarks from headings, heading levels from their size, the language of parts in another script, and ActualText with the reading order for right-to-left parts the page draws in visual order. Never writes alternate text. Result: `fixed`.
- **claim_ua**: marks a tagged PDF as PDF/UA-1 in its XMP metadata, after embedding the fonts formpdf has a match for and writing the title to XMP. Refused when the file has no tags, a font stays unembedded, or accessibility still finds a failed rule (figures without alternate text are the usual one). A PDF/A file keeps its PDF/A claim, and pdfa keeps a PDF/UA claim. Result: `fonts_embedded`.
- **edit_structure** `{changes: [{id, type, alt, decorative, move: up | down}]}`: `type` is P, H1 to H6, BlockQuote, Caption, Note, Code, Formula, Figure or Span; `alt` sets alternate text (empty removes it); `decorative: true` (or `type: "Artifact"`) takes the region out of the tags and marks its content as background; `move` swaps it with the region before or after it in the same parent. Ids come from structure on the same version; read structure again after each change.
- **pdfa** `{level: 2b | 2u | 3b}`: converts to PDF/A: removes what is forbidden (scripts, actions, multimedia, hidden comments; attached files except for 3b), embeds Liberation in place of fonts the file names but lacks (Helvetica, Times, Courier and similar), writes text mappings for 2u, converts CMYK to RGB, adds an sRGB output intent and PDF/A metadata, then checks again. Result: `conforms`, `fixed`, `fonts` (substitutions) and `remaining` issues, which converting cannot fix (for example characters with no text equivalent). Refused on a signed PDF.
- **pdfx** `{profile, condition}`: converts to PDF/X-4 with the printer's ICC output profile at `profile` (a path; formpdf ships none), embedding fonts, adding trim boxes and a DefaultRGB for RGB content when the profile is CMYK.
- **preflight_fix** `{fixes: [embed_fonts, hairlines, trim_box, scripts, downsample, gray], ppi}`: applies the named fixups; `downsample` takes pictures above one and a half times `ppi` (default 150) down to it.
- **run_list** `{list | steps: [{action, args}]}`: runs an action list, each step on the previous step's result, as one change. `list` is the name or id of a built-in list (Prepare for distribution, Optimise scans, Mark confidential, Make accessible, Archive as PDF/A) or one the person saved in the window (Tools > Action lists); `steps` runs an ad hoc list of read and change actions (at most 40; export actions and run_list are refused). Stops at the first failing step. Result: `pages`, `steps` (each step's label, text and time). Plug-ins the person has turned on add their own actions, which appear in this list and in get_context like any other.
- **import_xfdf** `{xfdf | data}`: adds the comments of an XFDF file (`xfdf` a path, `data` its text) that this PDF does not have yet (matched by name), with replies, status, authors and dates, as one change; signatures stay valid. Result: `comments`, `replies`, `skipped`.
- **ai_picture** `{prompt, page, x, y, w, h}`: generates a picture with OpenAI or a picture server on the person's computer and places it as an editable page picture (centred, half the page wide, when x, y, w, h are omitted). Not available with Anthropic or your agent as provider.
- **edit_items** `{add, update, arrange, comments, remove}`: page text and pictures that stay editable. `add`: `{kind: "text", page, x, y, w, text}` (plain text, one paragraph per line) or `paras: [{align: left | center | right | justify, list: none | bullet | number, level: 0..3, start (number lists: the number this paragraph shows, so a list split across text boxes continues), runs: [{t, b, i, u, s, sup, sub, color}]}]`, with `font: {family: sans | serif | mono, size, color, lineHeight}` and `rot`; the height follows the text. `{kind: "picture", page, x, y, w, h, path | data, rot, flipH, flipV, crop: {l, t, r, b}}` (crop as fractions of the picture). `update`: `{id, ...}` with any of those fields, or `{page, picture: op}` to change a picture already on the page (it becomes an item, `converted` in the result gives its id); a new crop alone keeps the picture's scale; `path` or `data` replaces the picture in its frame. `{page, paragraph: key}` edits a paragraph already on the page: its glyphs leave the page's own content and it becomes page text with the same place, size, colour, alignment and line spacing, set in the document's own font where that font has every letter (the font's name is in `font.orig`) and in the closest bundled family otherwise; plain `text` keeps the paragraph's bold and italic, a one-line paragraph widens to fit, and text with no character mapping is refused. `arrange`: `[{id | page and picture | page and paragraph, to: front | back | forward | backward}]`. `comments`: `[{page, index, to}]` reorders comments. `remove`: ids, `{page, picture}` or `{page, paragraph}`. Result: `ids` of added items, `converted` ("page:op" or "page:tkey" to the new id).

## Export actions

- **split** `{ranges, out: existing folder}`: one file per range named `[stem]-part[n]-[range].pdf`. Default folder: beside the input, or `~/Formpdf/Exports` for the open PDF.
- **to_docx** `{pages, out}`: Word document with paragraphs in reading order, headings, bullet and numbered lists, pictures and tables; two- and three-column pages become Word columns, running headers and footers go into the Word header and footer with a live page number, and each page starts a new page.
- **to_html** `{pages, out, pictures}`: one self-contained web page, pictures inline, headings, lists and tables in reading order, running headers and footers left out.
- **to_rtf** `{pages, out, pictures}`: rich text with the same content, for WordPad, TextEdit and older word processors.
- **to_xlsx** `{pages, out, tables_only}`: one sheet per page in reading order; table numbers, money and percentages become real numbers with their formats. `tables_only` writes only the tables.
- **to_pptx** `{pages, out, text}`: PowerPoint with one slide per page at the first page's size; words become real text boxes (turned words rotated boxes, text inside form XObjects included), tables PowerPoint tables, and lines, shapes and pictures PowerPoint rectangles, connectors, freeforms and pictures, over a slide picture that holds only what could not be rebuilt (shadings, soft masks, clipped or skewed objects, and everything drawn before them). `text: false` gives pictures only. Result counts `textboxes`, `tables`, `shapes`, `pictures`.
- **export_xfdf** `{out, name}`: the comments with their replies and review status as an XFDF file, which Acrobat and other readers import.
- **export_changes** `{out, name}`: struck-out, replaced and inserted text and notes on text as a JSON change list (`format: formpdf-changes`), each change with its type (delete, replace, insert, comment), text, replacement, the 40 characters before and after it, page, author and review status. The script `docs/indesign/apply-formpdf-changes.jsx` in the formpdf folder applies it to the InDesign document the PDF came from.
- **ai_deck** `{outline | slides, brief, paths, out}`: a PowerPoint deck (16:9, title slide plus one slide per outline slide, sources in the speaker notes) from an outline (ai_outline's, edited if you like) or drafted on the spot.
- **accessibility_report** `{out, name}`: the accessibility check as a standalone HTML page (one table per group with each rule's status, reason and findings).
- **print_pdf** `{layout: pages | booklet | poster, nup: 1 | 2 | 4 | 6 | 9 | 16, order: horizontal | horizontal-reversed | vertical | vertical-reversed, border, mode: fit | shrink | actual | custom, percent, paper: auto | letter | legal | tabloid | a3 | a4 | a5 | {w, h}, orientation: auto | portrait | landscape, margin, binding: left | right, poster: {scale, overlap, marks, labels}, content: markups | document | stamps | fields, gray, pages, out}`: a new PDF of print sheets, ready for any printer. A booklet prints on both sides, flipping on the short edge. `gray` converts colours and pictures to gray in the file.
- **preflight_report** `{profile, out, name}`: the preflight result as a standalone HTML page.
