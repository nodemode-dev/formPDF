# demos/sign CREDITS

Every document the demo opens by default. Each ships as a base64 JS data pack in `assets/media/` so it works from file://.

| File in the demo | Title | Author | Source page | Licence as stated | What was changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `telescope-loan-agreement.pdf` (pack `loan-form.js`) | Telescope Loan Agreement, form TL-14 (fictional club) | formpdf project, generated by `tools/make-form.mjs` | not applicable, original work in this repository | Apache-2.0 (this repository's licence) | Drawn with pdf-lib in the Helvetica standard fonts; vector shapes only, no outside image, text or form. The club, address and people are invented. |
| `apollo11-mission-summary.pdf` (pack `apollo-summary.js`) | Apollo 11 Mission Report (NASA SP-238), Summary and Mission Description | NASA Manned Spacecraft Center, Mission Evaluation Team | https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19710015566 | "Work of the US Gov. Public Use Permitted." (as recorded for demo 1, which opened and read this page) | Byte-for-byte copy of `demo/assets/media/apollo-summary.js`: pages 13 to 24 of the 230 page report in a new PDF, document title set, cover page with the agency logo left out. |

NASA's own statement on reuse of its material is at https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/ ("generally are not subject to copyright in the United States"). NASA is acknowledged as the source of the report excerpt. Its text layer is the report's own character recognition.

Also bundled in the build, each with a row in the repository `NOTICE.md`: pdf.js (Apache-2.0), pdf-lib (MIT), Inter (SIL OFL 1.1) and Caveat (SIL OFL 1.1), used for the interface and the typed signature.
